Who speaks when

Newsreader upright: anything with a name. Collections, titles, the reader.

Newsreader italic: anything about a thing. Durations, sources, dates, counts, empty states.

Hanken Grotesk: anything you do. Buttons, filters, small card titles.

Icons: only back, search and more (⋯). Phosphor Light, 1.25px stroke, to match the serif.
