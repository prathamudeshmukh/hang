Design language, draft one

Four decisions combined on real screens: curious on a calm base, its own look with Android behaviors, a warm off-white wall that follows the system theme, and serif, sans and mono type. Still undecided and shown as placeholders: accent color (currently none), corner shape (currently 4px), motion, and icons.

What to look at

  • Does the off-white read as gallery wall, or still too warm or papery?
  • Do the mono "museum labels" feel like a nice detail, or like noise?
  • Is the serif light enough (weight 350), or should titles be heavier?
  • Is the near-black screening room right for the swipe feed, or should the feed stay on the wall color?
  • Next questions in the grilling: accent color, corner shape, motion, icons.
