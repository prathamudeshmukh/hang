Option 1

Cover stack

Each collection is a full-width cover, using its most recent image as the cover. Scrolling feels like walking past posters.

+ Image-led and immediate. − Only 3 or 4 collections fit on screen.

Option 2

Mosaic wall

A two-column grid where each collection is a small mosaic of three of its items, like Apple Photos albums.

+ Dense, familiar, and good with many collections. − The least surprising of the four.

Option 3

Today, then collections

Home works like a front page: recent saves, one item to rediscover, and then your collections. The Inbox lives inside "Recently saved".

+ Always something new at the top, and serendipity comes built in. − Collections sit further down the page.

Option 4

Exhibition rooms

Each collection fills the whole screen like a gallery room, with a few items hung on the wall. Swipe sideways to go to the next room, and tap to enter the grid.

+ The most "fun to explore", and it fits the Gallery idea. − Slow to jump to a specific collection once you have many.

Or combine them

For example: Option 3's "Recently saved" and "Rediscover" at the top, with Option 2's mosaic wall below. Annotate anything, or reply in the terminal.
