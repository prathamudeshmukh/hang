What kind of fun? Round two

Four directions that are nothing like the Desk or the Zine. They use the same content as before (Design inspo, Long reads, Cooking, Watch later). If none of them work, the most useful thing you can tell me is which app you already enjoy opening, or annotate any detail you like or hate.

E · fun comes from restraint

The Gallery

Quiet and editorial, like a museum. White space and a serif font, and your saved images and videos are the only color. It's fun the way a good gallery is: big, beautiful content and smooth, shared-element transitions as you move from the grid to an item.

In the spirit of: Are.na, Cosmos, Apple Photos memories

F · fun comes from exploring space

The Canvas

Your saves live on an infinite, zoomable map. Collections are clusters you pinch into, and items float as tiles you can drag around. Related items sit close together (AI places them by topic). Exploring is literally moving through space.

In the spirit of: Apple Freeform, Kosmik, a map app for your interests

The Issue

Each collection publishes itself as a magazine. Every week (or on demand) the app assembles your new saves into an "issue" with a generated cover, headlines and a pull quote. Browsing a collection means flipping through its back issues.

In the spirit of: Flipboard, The New Yorker app, a personal Sunday paper
