Three directions for the link-saving app. Each one shows the same collections and items on three screens: Home, a collection's masonry grid, and the swipe feed. Pick one to lead, or tell me what to steal from each. The cards and colors are placeholders, so judge the feeling, not the content.

The Desk

Your saves are physical things on a warm desk. Collections are stacks of polaroids with tape on them. Cards tilt a little, have weight, and spring back. You flick them to file them. Handwritten notes stand in for the AI's "why you saved this".

Direction D · leads with surprise

The Night Sky

The home screen isn't a list of collections. It's today's dig: something forgotten resurfaces each day. Untouched items gather "dust". The tone is calm and dark with a little magic, made for evening browsing.
