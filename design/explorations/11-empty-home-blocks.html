Empty Home: B, polished

Round 2 of #75's how-to, all in the Blocks family. Fixes from round 1: shapes are ink at low alpha (not rule grey), so they hold up in dark; the page has a real picture and a headline, so it reads as a page in another app, not a loading skeleton; the share sheet is lifted off the page. Each card also gets a 2× close-up.

B1 · Refined

Round 1's composition, cleaned up. Same page in all three prints; the share button gets a tap ring; the sheet rises with a preview line and a row of apps.

  • Closest to what you picked; fixes contrast and the skeleton read.
  • At 76 dp the share row is still small: five discs in 60 dp.

recommended

Each print frames only what matters for its step: the whole page, then the corner with the Share button at twice the size, then the share row with three big discs and the next one cut off at the edge (the row goes on, and More is past it).

B3 · The picture travels

B1, but the page's picture is a washed teal tile from the icon's palette, and it rides along: it's on the page, under the Share button, then as the preview at the top of the share sheet. The thing you share is the thing that ends up on your wall.

  • Tells the story without words; the only colours are the picture and Hang's mark.
  • Two colours on the wall instead of one; a step away from "mark is the only colour".
