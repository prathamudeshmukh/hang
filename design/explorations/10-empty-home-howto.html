Empty Home: the how-to

#75. Three ways to draw the three panels that hang where the strip goes when the wall is empty, and the waiting state (dashed frames, decided). Each is shown on the real Home at 1 dp = 1 px, light and dark. Prints are 76×98, the strip print size; captions are notes.

A · Line

recommended

Ink line drawings on the print's paper, like a curator's sketch pinned up. Pages are outlines; the share glyph is circled; the share row is outlined discs, and Hang's mark is the only colour on the wall.

  • Quietest; reads as part of a gallery, not a tutorial.
  • Thin lines at 76 dp: needs a 1.25 dp stroke minimum to survive dark mode.

B · Blocks

Filled shapes in rule grey, no outlines: the page is a stack of blocks, the share button a solid ink disc, the share row grey discs. Hang's mark again carries the only colour, so it pops harder than in A.

  • Bolder and more legible at strip size, especially in dark.
  • Closer to the icon's masonry language; risks looking like skeleton loading.

C · Wall text

No pictures: each print is a museum label with a large serif numeral and the step set inside it. Only print 3 has a picture, the mark. Captions under the prints go away; it may be under More sits under print 3.

  • Most on-voice with the typography; simplest to build and scale with font size.
  • Teaches less: nothing shows what the Share button or the share sheet look like.

Waiting

decided in grilling

First pull still running: Bringing your wall over… in the label slot, three dashed frames holding the strip's place, so nothing jumps when prints or the how-to arrive. Same for every direction.

To pick

  2. Hang's mark in panel 3: in colour (as drawn) or in ink like the other discs, with a ring?
